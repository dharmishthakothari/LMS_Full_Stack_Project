# LMS User Manual: Administrator Guide

**Document Version:** 1.1  
**Audience:** Institute Directors, Administrative Staff  

---

## 1. Accessing the Admin Portal
- Navigate to the LMS URL in Chrome, Edge, or Firefox.
- Enter registered Administrator email: `admin@lms.com` and password `Admin@123` (or use Fast Demo Login button).
- You will be greeted by the dynamic Institute KPI Dashboard.

## 2. Managing Users & Profiles
- Click **User Management** in the sidebar.
- Click **Add New User** to register faculty, staff, or students.
- Toggle user status between **Active** and **Deactivate** with one click. Inactive users are instantly locked out from API sessions.

## 3. Creating Courses & Syllabi
- Click **Courses** -> **Create New Course**.
- Input course code, category, duration in weeks, and tuition fee.
- Click into the course to build sequential **Course Modules** and add structured **Lectures**.

## 4. Batch Cohort Scheduling & Rosters
- Click **Batches & Rosters** -> **Create New Batch**.
- Specify batch start/end dates, class days (e.g. Mon, Wed, Fri), timings, assigned Instructor, and maximum capacity.
- Open batch details to view the enrolled student roster and enroll or drop students.

## 5. Fee Collection & Payment Receipts
- Click **Fee Payments** in the sidebar.
- Click **Record Payment** to log full or partial tuition fees with transaction references.
- Instantly download the official PDF Payment Receipt generated for the student.

## 6. Reports & Analytics Export
- Open **Reports & Analytics** to review monthly enrollment trends, course distribution, and batch fill rates.
- Click **Students CSV**, **Payments CSV**, or **Attendance CSV** to export institutional data.
