# LMS User Manual: Student & Learner Guide

**Document Version:** 1.1  
**Audience:** Enrolled Students  

---

## 1. Accessing Your Student Portal
- Sign in with student credentials (e.g., `student1@lms.com` / `Student@123`).
- Your Student Dashboard displays overall course progress meters, attendance percentage, pending assignments, and upcoming quizzes.

## 2. Attending Lectures & Accessing Study Materials
- Go to **My Courses & Syllabus** and choose your enrolled course.
- Click **Launch Classroom** on any lecture session.
- Watch embedded video streams, download reference PDF slides, and click **Mark as Completed** to advance your progress bar.

## 3. Submitting Assignments
- Go to **Assignments**.
- Inspect due dates and assignment instructions.
- Click **Submit Work**, attach your project ZIP/PDF, and click **Upload & Submit**.
- Once graded by your trainer, view your marks and instructor critique directly on the card.

## 4. Attempting Timed Quizzes
- Click **Quizzes & Tests**.
- Select an active test and click **Start Exam**.
- The exam interface loads with a live countdown timer. Navigate questions, select options, and submit your test.
- Your score and pass/fail result are calculated and displayed immediately.

## 5. Course Progress & Claiming Certificates
- Click **Course Progress** to view dynamic completion percentages across lectures, assignments, and attendance.
- When overall progress reaches 80% with attendance >= 75% and all fees cleared, click **Claim Certificate**.
- Download your official PDF Certificate from **My Certificates** and share your unique verification code.
