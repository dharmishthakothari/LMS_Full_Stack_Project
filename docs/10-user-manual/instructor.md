# LMS User Manual: Faculty & Instructor Guide

**Document Version:** 1.1  
**Audience:** Institute Faculty, Trainers, Academic Evaluators  

---

## 1. Accessing the Instructor Workspace
- Log in with faculty credentials (e.g., `dr.patel@lms.com` / `Instructor@123`).
- View assigned batches, active student counts, and pending assignment submissions on the Instructor Dashboard.

## 2. Managing Classroom Syllabi & Study Materials
- Navigate to **Courses & Syllabus**.
- Select your course to view syllabus modules.
- Click **Add Lecture** to provide lecture titles, descriptions, video URLs, and upload PDF/PPT slides for students to download.

## 3. Authoring & Grading Assignments
- Click **Assignments & Grading** -> **Create Assignment**.
- Set batch target, problem statement, maximum marks, and due date/time.
- When students submit deliverables, click **Review Submissions** -> **Evaluate**. Input marks obtained and personalized constructive feedback.

## 4. Authoring Timed MCQ Quizzes
- Click **Quizzes & Exams**.
- Author MCQ tests with question text, point weights, duration minutes, and correct answer flags.
- Quizzes become accessible to enrolled students within configured availability windows.

## 5. Recording Batch Attendance
- Click **Mark Attendance**.
- Select the target batch and session date.
- The student roster loads automatically. Toggle the button next to each student between **Present** and **Absent**.
- Click **Save Attendance Session** to commit the audit log.
